## Planning decisions (settling what the spec left to the builder)
1. **Proposals live on the booking** (no new table): `coaching_bookings` gains `reschedule_started_at`, `reschedule_way` (`propose`|`ask`), `proposal_starts_at`, `proposal_by` (`coach`|`viewer`), `proposal_at`, `proposal_note`, `cancel_reason`, `original_starts_at` (the time before the reschedule, for the "never the original time" rule). Only the latest proposal counts, as the spec says.
2. **Holding:** `rescheduling` joins `HOLDING` for the viewer's limits (MAX_UPCOMING), but the overlap check and free-time lists hold a rescheduling booking's `proposal_starts_at` (with its `minutes`) and **not** its `starts_at`. All held-time SQL (`_HOLD_SQL`, `createCoachingBooking`, `getHeldCoachingBookings`) uses one expression for "the time this row holds": `CASE WHEN status='rescheduling' THEN proposal_starts_at ELSE starts_at END` (a rescheduling row with no proposal holds nothing).
3. **Refunds per payment:** `coaching_payments` gains `payer_name`, `payer_email`, `payer_id`, `refund_status` (NULL, `pending`, `refunded`), `refund_reason` (`cancelled`, `not_rescheduled`, `late`, `double`), `refund_pending_at`, `refunded_at`. A coach cancel or a reschedule closing without agreement marks every captured, not-yet-refunded payment of that booking `pending` (an old payment without payer details still gets the step; the schedule then shows only its capture id). A `late_issue` or `double` capture marks its own payment `pending` at the moment it is recorded (only new captures: no backfill of rows that exist today).
4. **Way 2 link:** the viewer's Discord message links to `/coaching/<twitch login>?booking=<id>`; the booking page already lists "Your bookings" once signed in and shows the reschedule panel on that booking.
5. **A proposal coming within 1 hour** is dropped like a lapse: a coach's proposal → the viewer is asked to pick (way 2 message); a viewer's → the coach is reminded.
6. **Room on agreement:** the role and voice channel are renamed with Discord's PATCH (new helpers in `src/discord.js`), and `coaching_rooms.close_at` moves to the new end + 30 min. A rename that fails is logged and leaves the room working (never fails the agreement). `closeDue` skips a room whose booking is `rescheduling` (the close is paused, spec point 3).
7. **Rate limit:** the viewer's new reschedule routes on `/coaching/:username/bookings/:id/…` get their own limiter, `coachingRescheduleLimiter`, 30 per 10 minutes per visitor (`byVisitor`), next to `coachingPayLimiter` in `server.js`. The coach's routes sit behind dashboard login + CSRF like today's accept/decline/cancel.
8. **Ended sessions show no room:** `roomFields` (`coachingBookings.js` ~29) returns a room link only for `confirmed` (and `rescheduling`, whose room is kept) bookings. Cancelling a confirmed or rescheduling booking deletes its room and role at once through a new exported `coachingRooms.removeFor(bookingId)` (no transcript, state `closed`, transcript_status `cancelled`).
