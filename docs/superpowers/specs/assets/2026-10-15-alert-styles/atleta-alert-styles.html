Families

A family gives all six alerts one look. Streamers pick per alert, or set a whole family in one click.

    On top of any look

    These layers work with every look, so a streamer can put tyre smoke behind the samurai gate or give the Cockpit dash an arcade coin sound. Today’s screen effects (confetti, gold, money, robots, tyre marks) stay available.

    A sound for every look

    Each look comes with its own sound, made in the browser like today’s engine revs, so there is nothing to upload. A streamer’s own uploaded sound still wins. Click to listen.

    Grows with the amount

    Looks tagged in purple read the number in the alert. Switch the Example control above between Small, Medium and Big to see the difference.

    Notes

    Stays the same

    • The six looks you named become presets with exactly those names: follow-samurai, subscriber-samurai, bits-samurai, donation-samurai, raid-samurai, giveaway-racing.
    • Nobody’s overlay changes on release. An alert with no style chosen keeps today’s look.
    • Position and width still come from Size & Position, for every look.
    • The OBS link stays the same, and a newly saved style reaches an open overlay without a reload.

    Decided

    • Build all three new families: Grand Prix, Cockpit and Arcade, 18 looks.
    • The racing cards come back for all six alerts, improved: follow-racing to giveaway-racing. The giveaway’s card is improved in place; a streamer’s saved colours and texts carry over.
    • Quest, Comic, Cozy, Cosmos and Glass stay as they are.
    • The omikuji joins the samurai family as its giveaway, unless you’d rather leave it out.

    New in this proposal

    • Racing, redone: a headlight streak flies past and leaves a carbon panel behind it; then a race number, nitro, a sponsor decal, headlights and a victory lap. Still every colour, font and text editable, and still today’s colours by default.
    • Quest, tidied: one move at a time and nothing blurred in. The scrolls show their writing as they open, the XP bar fills before the level-up, the raid’s banners rise from behind the board, the decree’s rod and parchment move together, and the loot card really turns over.
    • Eight more families with nothing to do with racing: Spooky (playful Halloween), Cyber (neon cyberpunk), Western, Retro OS (a 90s desktop), Graffiti, Kawaii (cute pastel), Steampunk and Frost (winter and festive), 48 looks.
    • Pick the ones to keep. Each family ships on its own, so any of them can wait for a later release.

    Found while preparing this

    • Today’s giveaway card has no look of its own. Its colours come only from a design saved in the builder. Without one it is see-through, with a black label and border (checked against overlay.css). The improved giveaway-racing always has its colours.
    • The samurai looks are fixed to Ronin red. They could take a colour the same way the new families do, as a later step.
    • The overlay page sizes every box with its padding inside (box-sizing: border-box in overlay.css). This page now does the same, so what you see here is what OBS draws; the families drawn the other way say so in their own styles and look exactly as before.
